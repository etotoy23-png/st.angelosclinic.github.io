Overview of the Clinic

St. Angelo's Multi Specialist and Diagnostic Center proudly serves the primary communities of Biñan, Laguna. Our medical care and diagnostic outreach extend closely to proximate towns including Muntinlupa City (Metro Manila), San Pedro, GMA Cavite, Carmona, Sta. Rosa, and Cabuyao. Over the past 27 years, our commitment to patient-first service has been well accepted and recognized across the region.

Mission, Vision & Objectives

Our Mission

To provide proper, cost-effective laboratory testing and impart superior customer service in an environment that promotes compassionate care and contributes to co-worker satisfaction. We strive to grow and adapt to meet the needs of our patients.


Our Vision

To be a prime healthcare provider in the region that we serve; to be recognized for its devotion and pursuit for excellence in delivering quality healthcare services.

Core Objectives

  • Guarantee that all medical equipment related to laboratory testing activities are properly maintained and monitored.
  • Deliver accurate test results on the given time frame.
  • Provide accurate and precise test results that may help in diagnosis of certain diseases.
  • Keep up with current trends of the methodologies and procedures of laboratory medicine.
