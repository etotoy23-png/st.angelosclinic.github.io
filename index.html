Our Accredited Departments

Secondary Laboratory

Regulated and accredited under Department of Health standards(such as DOH-mandated proficiency testing and guidelines) to ensure accurate chain-of-custody and analytical reliability.

Screening Drug Test Laboratory

DOH accredited on-site Screening examinations. A medical facility uses immunoassay methods to test urine or other body fluids for common substances of abuse. These tests support pre-employment, school entry, and legal or corporate compliance.

X-Ray Department

FDA accredited on-site medical imaging framework facilitating fast, high-quality diagnostic examinations.

12-lead ECG (electrocardiogram)

a quick, painless, non-invasive test that records the heart's electrical activity from 12 different angles using 10 physical skin electrodes. It helps doctors diagnose heart attacks, irregular heartbeats (arrhythmias), and other cardiac issues.

Ultrasound Machine

Advanced internal imaging systems deployed actively for medical, diagnostic, and employment use.

Audiometry

a safe, painless hearing test that measures your ability to hear sounds by their volume (intensity in decibels) and pitch (frequency in Hertz). It helps detect hearing loss and identify its type and underlying cause.

Industrial Medical Services

Specialized occupational health programs, workplace injury treatments, and routine employee health exams. These services help companies maintain safe environments and comply with labor health standards.

Pre-Employment Exams

Custom screening solutions continuously integrated to meet the unique corporate health needs of local communities.
