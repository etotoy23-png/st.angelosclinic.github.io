Overview of the Clinic

Over the past 27 years, our commitment to patient-first service has been well accepted and recognized across the region. We feature an on-site DOH-accredited secondary laboratory, screening drug test laboratory, FDA-accredited X-ray department, and ultrasound capabilities to meet individual and corporate health requirements.

Our Accredited Departments

Secondary Laboratory

Regulated and accredited under Department of Health standards to ensure accurate chain-of-custody and analytical reliability.

Screening Drug Test Laboratory

DOH-accredited on-site screening examinations utilizing immunoassay methods for pre-employment, school entry, and corporate compliance.

X-Ray Department

FDA-accredited on-site medical imaging framework facilitating fast, high-quality diagnostic examinations.

12-Lead ECG

Quick, painless, non-invasive test recording heart electrical activity from 12 angles to help diagnose cardiac issues.

Ultrasound Machine

Advanced internal imaging systems deployed actively for medical, diagnostic, and employment use.

Audiometry

Safe, painless hearing test that measures ability to hear sounds by volume and pitch to detect potential hearing loss.

Industrial Medical Services

Specialized occupational health programs, workplace injury treatments, and routine employee health exams.

Pre-Employment Exams

Custom screening solutions continuously integrated to meet corporate health standards of local communities.

Mission, Vision & Objectives

Our Mission

To provide proper, cost-effective laboratory testing and impart superior customer service in an environment that promotes compassionate care and contributes to co-worker satisfaction. We strive to grow and adapt to meet the needs of our patients.


Our Vision

To be a prime healthcare provider in the region that we serve; to be recognized for its devotion and pursuit of excellence in delivering quality healthcare services.

Core Objectives

  • Guarantee that all medical equipment related to laboratory testing activities are properly maintained and monitored.
  • Deliver accurate test results on the given time frame.
  • Provide accurate and precise test results that may help in the diagnosis of certain diseases.
  • Keep up with current trends of the methodologies and procedures of laboratory medicine.
